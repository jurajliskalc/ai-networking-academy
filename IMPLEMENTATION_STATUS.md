## Known limitations

* The simulator is a teaching model: absolute numbers are modelled, not measured. Host-tool output formats
  follow captures from ConnectX-5 hardware (DOCA-OFED 3.4); InfiniBand-mode and switch output follow tool
  sources and documentation.
* Exam and quiz content reflects public information as of 2026; vendor product details change quickly.
* Browser tests render every widget but drive the interactions of 14 of 45 (see NEXT_STEPS.md).
