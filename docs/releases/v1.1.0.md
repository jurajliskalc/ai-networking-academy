# AI Networking Academy 1.1.0

Figures drawn by the academy's own renderer, and a five-minute tour in pictures.

**Start here:** [live academy](https://jurajliskalc.github.io/ai-networking-academy/) ·
[five-minute tour](https://github.com/jurajliskalc/ai-networking-academy/blob/main/docs/PORTFOLIO_TOUR.md)

## Figures

- The 84 figures in the lessons are now laid out at build time and drawn as SVG in the app's colour scheme:
  orthogonal connections, labels on their lines, grouped boxes, and tints for decisions, outcomes, starts
  and ends. Their content is unchanged.
- Figures that show a path flow for a few seconds when they come into view, and sequence diagrams can be
  played step by step. Neither runs with the "reduce motion" setting on.
- Mermaid is no longer shipped with the app, which makes it smaller and removes layout work from the
  browser.

## Fixed

- With the OS "reduce motion" setting on, every flowchart was drawn collapsed and unreadable.
- Long inline code (paths, counter names) made ten lesson pages scroll sideways on a phone; it now wraps.

## Changed

- The five-minute tour is a highlights page in pictures: an incident solved end to end, visualizations in
  motion, notes from real hardware, the same commands on two driver stacks, a quiz question and the fabric
  health review.
- The lesson "Interview preparation" is titled "Technical interviews and design reviews". Its ID is
  unchanged, so progress and links keep working.
- The README leads with the live academy and links the simulator labs and the visualizations.

## How the figure change was checked

- The new parser reads the same boxes, connections, groups and message order that Mermaid read from every
  figure before it was replaced; that comparison is a permanent test.
- For every figure, tests check that the labels drawn equal the labels written, that no boxes overlap, that
  text fits its box and that connections reach their boxes.
- In Chrome, in light and dark, at desktop and phone width: no label outside its box, no overlaps and no
  page scrolling sideways. Three figures are wider than the lesson column on a desktop and scroll inside
  their frame.

Hardware verification status and known limitations are unchanged from
[1.0.0](https://github.com/jurajliskalc/ai-networking-academy/releases/tag/v1.0.0); open work is in the
[roadmap](https://github.com/jurajliskalc/ai-networking-academy/blob/main/ROADMAP.md).
