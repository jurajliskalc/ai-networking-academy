# AI Networking Academy 1.0.0

First public release: a hands-on training and validation platform for AI cluster networking. It runs
entirely in the browser, and a toolkit extends it to your own RDMA hardware.

**Start here:** [live academy](https://jurajliskalc.github.io/ai-networking-academy/) ·
[the 5-minute PFC incident](https://jurajliskalc.github.io/ai-networking-academy/#/labs/sim-03-pfc-priority-mismatch) ·
[portfolio tour](https://github.com/jurajliskalc/ai-networking-academy/blob/main/docs/PORTFOLIO_TOUR.md)

## Curriculum

- 76 lessons in 13 modules and four levels, from the Ethernet frame to a 1,024-GPU fabric design: RDMA,
  InfiniBand, RoCEv2 and lossless Ethernet, load balancing and congestion control, collectives and NCCL,
  cluster design, operations and troubleshooting.
- 568 quiz questions with every option explained, and 3 timed mock exams (156 questions) scored by domain.
  Original practice material, not real certification questions.
- 45 interactive visualizations, a reference of 165 annotated commands and a 241-term glossary with
  spaced-repetition flashcards.

## Fabric Simulator

- A deterministic in-browser model of a 32-GPU rail-optimized RoCE cluster (4 nodes, 8 rails, 8 leaves,
  2 spines) and an InfiniBand variant, with a shell that speaks real tool syntax.
- 22 simulator labs: incident tickets with injected faults (MTU black holes, PFC and trust
  misconfigurations, lost switch QoS, marginal optics, PCIe downtraining, GID errors, NCCL drift, low ECMP
  entropy, subnet manager failures). A lab is solved only when the diagnosis is right and the fabric passes
  every check.
- Every lab is replayed by the test suite (broken before its documented fix, clean after it), and host-tool
  output formats are compared with captures from real adapters.

## Hands-on and real-hardware labs

- 22 hands-on labs in four tiers: 7 on one Ubuntu VM (Soft-RoCE, containerlab Clos with BGP unnumbered,
  EVPN-VXLAN, ECMP, ECN) and 15 on your own hardware (RoCE bring-up and QoS, break/fix with fault
  injection, InfiniBand, subnet manager failover, IPoIB, partitions, MPI over UCX, and more).
- `aina-hw`, a dependency-free Python toolkit: host inventory over SSH, lab checks that are graded on the
  measurements they require, a read-only local agent, live port counters and a fabric health review in the
  browser.

## Hardware verification status

- Reference hardware: two Dell PowerEdge R640 hosts with ConnectX-5 VPI adapters, 100GbE RoCE and EDR
  InfiniBand cabled back to back, Ubuntu 26.04. RoCE was checked on both the distribution's inbox RDMA
  stack and NVIDIA DOCA-OFED 3.4, InfiniBand on DOCA-OFED.
- 19 of the 22 hands-on labs have been run end to end and are marked verified: all seven Tier 0 labs and
  twelve hardware labs (`hw-01` to `hw-05`, `hw-09` to `hw-15`).
- Host commands and sample output across the content and the simulator were compared with that hardware.
  259 output blocks are real captures tagged by driver stack, and 32 observations are recorded as verified
  notes. The reference is
  [docs/HARDWARE_VERIFICATION.md](https://github.com/jurajliskalc/ai-networking-academy/blob/main/docs/HARDWARE_VERIFICATION.md).

## Known limitations

- `hw-06` (needs a lossless switch) and `hw-07`, `hw-08` (need GPU nodes) are written but not run, and stay
  `verified: false`.
- Not verified on hardware: switch CLIs (NVUE, SONiC), switched-fabric behaviour, 400G-specific and
  optical-module output, and GPU or NCCL output. These follow primary sources and are labelled illustrative.
- The reference setup is lab scale: one NIC model, no switch, no GPUs, inside VMs with PCI passthrough.
  Observations are stated for that hardware, not as general vendor behaviour.
- The simulator is a teaching model. Its absolute numbers are modelled, not measured, and it is not a
  performance predictor.
- The content was produced with AI coding agents and checked by AI review passes, primary sources, tests
  and hardware runs. None of those review passes is a human peer review; corrections with a source are
  welcome.
- Accessibility is checked with axe in CI; it has not been tested with a screen reader.
- Vendor product details reflect public information as of 2026 and change quickly.
